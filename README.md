I've always liked puzzles, details, and exploring new challenges. I spent 28+ years coaching, and two decades running consultancies in human performance, design and digital transformation, and organisational strategy and governance. In 2025 I co-founded **mixOmics Pro** with Professor [Kim-Anh Lê Cao](https://github.com/kimanh-lecao). I help biological scientists whose progress is bottlenecked by limited tools, complex workflows, and inaccessible expertise.
